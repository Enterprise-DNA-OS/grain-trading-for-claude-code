---
description: The trading margin on grain out-turned: sold price, average buy price, freight, and margin a tonne and in total, by grade and season.
---

1. Run `npm run grain -- margin`.
2. Present by grade: tonnes sold, sold and bought price a tonne, freight a tonne, margin a tonne and in dollars.
3. Flag any grade with a negative margin and say why from the numbers (bought high, freight high, sold low).
4. Levies and royalties come out of the grower's settlement, so they are not in the margin. Brokerage is on the contract (`npm run grain -- contract <number>`).
