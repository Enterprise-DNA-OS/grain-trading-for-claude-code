---
description: The position by grade and season: stock in store, bought and still to come, sold and still to go, and the net long or short, with today's cash price.
---

1. Run `npm run grain -- position`. For what is physically in store by site: `npm run grain -- stock`.
2. Lead with any grade short: sold more than is in store and bought. Say how many tonnes, and what it would cost to buy them at today's cash price.
3. Then the biggest long positions, and the cash price against them.
4. For what the book is worth at today's prices, run /mtm.
