# Compliance: the rules this system checks

`/compliance` (or `npm run grain -- compliance`) checks the records against the rules below and reports every breach with the rule and its source. They are written for an Australian grain trader, accumulator or private storage site buying from growers and settling on recipient created tax invoices. Set the numbers to yours when you install it: the RCTI window (`npm run grain -- settings --rcti-days=28`), each commodity's levy rate (`npm run grain -- commodity WHT --levy=1.02`), each variety's end point royalty, and the receival spec of each grade.

This is a records check. It finds what the records say. It is not legal or tax advice, and it does not replace your accountant, the ATO, the department, your EPR managers, your bulk handler's standards or the NHVR. Confirm each rule against the current version of its source before you rely on it, and when a rule changes, change the setting or the check (`complianceRows` in `scripts/grain.mjs`) and this page in the same edit.

| Rule | What a breach looks like in the data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 RCTI agreement in force | An RCTI drafted, issued or paid for a grower with no RCTI agreement on file, one that started after or ended before the RCTI date, a grower not registered for GST, or no ABN | The ATO's tax invoices page: you can issue an RCTI only if you and the supplier are both registered for GST, you have agreed in writing that you may issue RCTIs and they will not issue tax invoices, the agreement is current when you issue it, and the supply is of a kind the Commissioner has determined can be invoiced by RCTI (Recipient Created Tax Invoice Determination 2023) ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/tax-invoices)). GSTR 2000/10 adds that the words "recipient created tax invoice" and both ABNs must be shown ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/pdf?DocId=GST%2FGSTR200010%2FNAT%2FATO%2F00001&filename=law%2Fview%2Fpdf%2Fpbr%2Fgstr2000-010c5.pdf&PiT=20090603000001)) |
| R2 RCTI within 28 days | A load on a contract with no issued RCTI more than `rcti_days` (default 28) after it was delivered, or an RCTI issued more than 28 days after a load on it | The RCTI determinations require the recipient to issue the RCTI to the supplier within 28 days of making, or determining, the value of the supply (see the ATO determinations, for example [rcti2016-024](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/pdf/ldt/rcti2016-024.pdf)). The check counts from the delivery date; if your contracts set the value later (a pool, a pricing window), count from that date instead |
| R3 Levy withheld | A settled load of a levied commodity whose levy is more than a cent under its value times the commodity's levy rate | The Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry sets the rate. Wheat is 1.02% of the net value (0.99% research and development, 0.015% National Residue Survey, 0.01% Plant Health Australia, 0.005% emergency plant pest response), net of GST, handling, storage, transport and FOB costs ([agriculture.gov.au](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/agriculture-land/farm-food-drought/levies/rates/wheat)). The demo uses 1.02% for barley and canola too: check each commodity's own page before you lodge |
| R4 Commodity vendor declaration | A load received with no vendor declaration recorded | Grain Trade Australia's Technical Guideline No. 14: a CVD travels with the grain each time it changes hands, and is kept long enough to trace the grain back to its supplier ([graintrade.org.au](https://graintrade.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/TGD-No.14-Commodity-Vendor-Declaration_May2018.pdf)). The GTA form covers chemical treatments, withholding periods and GMO status ([graintrade.org.au](https://graintrade.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/GTA-Commodity-Vendor-Declaration_revisied_Oct16.pdf)) |
| R5 Variety declared | A load received of a commodity with end point royalties and no variety recorded | End point royalties are collected under the Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994 on protected varieties: the grower declares the variety at the first point of sale and the buyer deducts the royalty and remits it to the breeder or its agent ([plantsuccess.org](https://www.plantsuccess.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/18-End-Point-Royalties-Fact-Sheet.pdf), [seednet.com.au](https://www.seednet.com.au/royalties)) |
| R6 Written contract confirmation | A contract with loads against it and no confirmation date, or a confirmation dated after the first load | Grain Trade Australia: a contract made on the phone is customarily confirmed by a written Contract Confirmation, which the seller should check straight away; without a notice of a discrepancy the buyer may take the contract to be as written ([graintrade.org.au](https://graintrade.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Producer_QandA_Update_03092018.pdf)). The GTA Trade Rules apply unless the contract varies them ([graintrade.org.au](https://graintrade.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/GTA-Trade-Rules_BoardApproved_27May25.pdf)) |
| R7 Load meets its grade | A load whose protein, moisture, screenings, test weight or oil is outside the spec of the grade it was received or out-turned at | The receival standards for the season, from your bulk handler, your buyer's contract or the industry standards your contracts name. The specs in the demo data are illustrative: load your own before you rely on this check. Under the GTA Trade Rules, quality claims are notified within five business days of unloading ([graintrade.org.au](https://graintrade.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/GTA-Trade-Rules_BoardApproved_27May25.pdf)) |
| R8 Truck within its mass limit | A load whose gross weight is more than the mass limit the driver declared | Heavy Vehicle National Law section 26C puts a duty on every party in the chain of responsibility, receivers and loading managers included, not to cause or encourage a breach of mass limits. Grain harvest management schemes ask participating receivers to keep verified weighbridges and a process for overloaded vehicles; Viterra records the driver's declared mass limit on the weighnote ([viterra.com.au](https://viterra.com.au/Growers/Delivering-and-storing/Making-deliveries/Driver-information)). Harvest concessions vary by state and season: set the limit you record to the one that applied |

## Why each one matters

- **R1 and R2.** An RCTI issued without an agreement in force, or late, is a tax invoice the ATO may not accept, and the GST credit behind it is at risk. The grower also waits for money they are owed.
- **R3.** Levy not withheld is still owed on the return. It comes out of the trader's margin if nobody catches it before the quarter closes.
- **R4.** A load with no vendor declaration cannot be traced if a residue turns up in a shipment, and one bad shipment can close a market.
- **R5.** A load with no variety has no royalty deducted, and the EPR manager audits deliveries against declarations.
- **R6.** A phone deal with no written confirmation is a dispute waiting for a falling market.
- **R7.** A load paid as APW1 that tests as ASW1 is money paid for protein you did not get, and it is in the bin with grain that does meet spec.
- **R8.** An overloaded truck that leaves your weighbridge with a ticket is evidence your site saw it.

## Changing a rule

Ask in plain words ("our RCTIs go out within 14 days", "canola levy is a different rate", "harvest concession is 5% over general mass this season"). Claude Code changes the setting or the check and this page together, and runs `npm test`.
